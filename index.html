How to play Tüppen

A four-card hand, three players, and one decisive trick.

  1. Each round begins with a four-card deal from a 32-card deck.
  2. The Seven of Diamonds must lead the opening trick.
  3. Before play begins, every player may Knock (Klopfen), Stand (Behalten), or Pass (Passen). If everyone else folds, the knocker wins the round immediately.
  4. Otherwise the table plays four tricks in classic order. Players follow suit whenever they can.
  5. Whoever plays the King of Clubs on the fourth trick claims the entire round and every outstanding point.
