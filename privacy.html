Short version:

Tuppen Royale is a fully offline card game. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Your photos, statistics, achievements, and match history are stored locally on your device and never leave it. There is no account, no sign-up, and no network gameplay.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device

Tuppen Royale does not collect any personal data from you. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Account credentials or authentication tokens
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Photos, portraits, or card-back images (they stay on your device)

All game data — including your profile photo, custom card backs, opponent names, statistics, achievements, and match history — is stored locally using standard on-device storage provided by iOS. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions we request

iOS may ask you to grant the following permissions. Each is entirely optional and only used for the feature described.

  • Photo Library (read): So you can pick a portrait for your seat or an image for a custom card back. The image you select stays on your device.
  • Camera: So you can take a new portrait or a card-back photo with your camera. Photos you capture are saved locally; we never upload them anywhere.
  • Photo Library (add): If you choose to save a victory screenshot to your photo library via the system share sheet.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. The app continues to work without them, with reduced functionality.

Third-party services

Tuppen Royale does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or social-networking SDKs. The app does not contact any remote server during normal use.

Apple's App Store and iOS may collect diagnostics and usage data according to Apple's own privacy policies. Those collections are governed by Apple's privacy terms, not this one.

Children's privacy

Tuppen Royale is suitable for all ages and does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age under local law).

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through an in-app notice. Because we do not collect contact information, we may ask you to review the policy when you next open the app.
